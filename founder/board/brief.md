# Board brief: Edurance

This brief is everything the board knows. Treat anything marked "assumption" or "not yet known" as unproven.

## The idea

Edurance is an AI-powered learning platform that gives school students personalised education. Diagnostic assessments and learning data are used to understand each student's level, strengths, weaknesses, pace and knowledge gaps. The platform combines adaptive learning, AI-powered explanations, voice-based learning (text-to-speech narration), personalised learning paths, progress and mastery tracking, and visibility for parents and schools. The long-term vision is an AI tutor that keeps adapting to each student and makes personalised learning more accessible and scalable.

## Customers

- Users: school students.
- Key customer and distribution channel: schools.
- Stakeholders and possible subscription decision-makers: parents.
- The first focus is partnering with schools to bring Edurance to groups of students, rather than relying mainly on acquiring individual students.

## Market

- Starting market for school acquisition: Hyderabad and Secunderabad, Telangana, India.
- The go-to-market plan maps a prospect universe of 150 schools across four geographic zones, each managed by a founder.
- Expansion into other markets depends on validation and available resources.

## Price

- ₹299 per paid user on the current subscription model. The billing period (monthly, per term, per year) has not been stated.
- Not yet known: actual willingness to pay, conversion rate, the revenue share arrangement with schools, and net revenue per user.

## Product and stage (as stated by the founders)

- A working web-first product built with Flutter, Supabase, OpenAI APIs and Vercel.
- Existing capabilities: authentication, diagnostic tests with saved results, lesson resumption, adaptive teaching flows, AI-powered explanations, text-to-speech narration, audio replay and caching, and features for parents.
- The company is working towards validating school acquisition, pilot delivery, student activation, engagement, retention and paid conversion.

## Go-to-market

School-led B2B2C. Planned journey: school outreach → meetings and demos with decision-makers → proposals → signed pilots → educator orientation → student onboarding → baseline diagnostics → learning activation → progress review → paid conversion and expansion.

The October plan has the 150-school prospect universe and working funnel targets. These are planning assumptions, not proven conversion rates or confirmed customers.

## Team

Four co-founders, with responsibilities across technology, business and partnerships, operations, and product. The current role allocation has not been confirmed.

## Budget

Not specified. Do not assume a budget. Prefer low-cost validation.

## Not yet known (do not invent these)

The startup budget; current school commitments or signed pilots; actual user numbers; activation, engagement and retention; measured learning outcomes; operating costs, including AI/API cost per student; conversion rates at any stage; the school revenue share; competitors in this market.

## The founders' own biggest uncertainties

1. Will schools adopt Edurance and commit to paid or structured pilots?
2. Will students keep using the product after onboarding?
3. Will parents and schools see enough value to justify ₹299 per paid user?
4. Does Edurance improve learning outcomes or meaningfully close learning gaps?
5. What share of school prospects will become signed pilots and paying users?
6. Can the team handle onboarding, support and product reliability as adoption grows?
7. Can the business acquire and keep users economically, after the school revenue share and operating costs?
