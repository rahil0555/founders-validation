# Edurance

- What it is: An AI-powered adaptive learning platform that diagnoses each school student's level, strengths, gaps and pace, then teaches them on a personalised path with AI explanations, voice narration and mastery tracking, with visibility for parents and schools.
- Who it is for: School students are the users. Schools are the key customer and distribution channel; parents are stakeholders and possible subscription decision-makers.
- What it sells, at what price: A student subscription at ₹299 per paid user (billing period not yet stated). Willingness to pay, conversion, the school revenue share and net revenue per user are not yet validated.
- Where and how: Hyderabad and Secunderabad, Telangana, India, to start. School-led B2B2C: outreach → meetings and demos → proposals → signed pilots → educator orientation → student onboarding → baseline diagnostics → learning activation → progress review → paid conversion and expansion. Web-first product.
- Budget and constraints: Startup budget not specified. Four co-founders (technology, business and partnerships, operations, product; allocation to be confirmed). A working product already exists.
